How this maps to a real AI red-team program

The three tabs mirror a real workflow. Arena is manual exploration: a human probes one target and learns its weakness. Auto Red-Team is the automated sweep that tools like garak and PyRIT run, firing a battery of probes at every target and recording what lands. Dashboard is the report: coverage and findings rolled up to OWASP LLM Top 10 so owners can prioritise.

Everything here is simulated with hard-coded rules and benign codewords, so results are deterministic and transfer to no live system. The value is the method: enumerate techniques, sweep targets, map findings to a framework, fix by category. Take that to models you are authorized to test.
